🏗️ Smart Waste System Architecture

Hackathon-ready blueprint for an AI and IoT based smart waste management platform.

PROTOTYPE READY
IoT
Sensor-enabled smart bins with fill, gas, moisture, vibration, and temperature telemetry
AI
Waste image classification, stream detection, and municipal decision support
Ops
Route prioritization, overflow alerts, dashboards, and exportable records
1. Smart Bin Layer

Each smart bin can publish fill level, gas concentration, moisture, vibration, and temperature data. A camera or citizen upload can classify waste into recyclable, biodegradable, hazardous, or residual streams.

2. Data & Communication Layer

Sensor packets are ingested through wireless links such as Wi-Fi, GSM, or LoRa. The backend stores live readings, waste events, historical activity, and bin states for real-time dashboards and planning.

3. AI & Analytics Layer
  • Computer vision for waste-type and waste-stream detection
  • Predictive trend analysis of waste generation and bin utilization
  • Priority scoring and route optimization for collection fleets
  • Anomaly and overflow alert generation for authorities
4. Operations & User Interface
  • Command center dashboard for municipal staff
  • IoT monitoring view for distributed bins
  • Alert center for critical events and irregularities
  • Analytics and environmental impact reporting
Prototype Use Case Flow
1. Detect

Bin sensors and AI image uploads capture real-time waste data.

2. Classify

Waste is categorized into recyclable, biodegradable, hazardous, or residual streams.

3. Prioritize

Critical bins are scored by urgency, weight, and operational risk.

4. Optimize

Collection recommendations and route plans are generated for municipal teams.

Scalability Plan

The system is designed for city-wide deployment with distributed smart bins, stateless APIs, modular AI services, and database-backed dashboards. Additional zones, vehicles, and waste streams can be added without changing the user workflow.

Expected Deliverables Covered
  • Functional prototype / MVP
  • System architecture and design view
  • AI waste analytics and monitoring demo
  • Collection optimization and alerts
  • Scalability and smart-city deployment narrative
